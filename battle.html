What could change the answer?

Body size, environment, available space, ambush, individual variation and specialized weapons can overturn a rating-only estimate. The preview does not quantify these effects. It should not be used to compare land and marine fighting conditions as though they were equivalent.

Community voting is a separate signal. Open the comparison arena to explore the existing community tools.
