# Image delivery audit and backfill queue

**Research reports completed:** 185 / 225 (through Sawfish). **Full-body transparent PNG assets verified and committed:** 0 / 185. A source-photo URL is not an approved PNG.

`IMAGE_REQUIREMENTS.md` is mandatory and retroactive. Exact species, healthy adult, complete anatomy, real-photo provenance, adequate quality and genuine alpha transparency are required. Current connected GitHub write actions are UTF-8 text-only, so source photos remain pending rather than being faked as PNGs.

## Queue policy
Animals already source-verified are skipped until binary tooling becomes available. The oldest unresolved source-quality candidate is addressed each run. `REPLACEMENT SOURCE NEEDED`, `SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED`, and licensing blockers remain unresolved until a genuinely better source is found.

## 2026-09-23 Lynx legacy audit
Roster Lynx resolves to Eurasian lynx ***Lynx lynx***, with a healthy fully mature adult male preferred because males are materially larger.

The existing report candidate was re-opened at original resolution:
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Male_Lynx_lynx_resting_in_tree.jpg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/6b/Male_Lynx_lynx_resting_in_tree.jpg
- **5184x3456, CC BY-SA 4.0**, exact *L. lynx*, explicitly male.
- Direct visual inspection confirms that the tree/branch pose hides and overlaps substantial portions of the limbs and underside. It fails the mandatory clean whole-body silhouette standard; adulthood is also not explicit.

Fresh broad search found a much stronger standing exact-species candidate:
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lynx_lynx_carpathicus_Germany_(standing).jpg
- Original image page provides the 1543x1336 original; source observation: https://www.inaturalist.org/photos/246545089
- **1543x1336, CC BY 4.0**, exact *Lynx lynx carpathicus*, side/three-quarter standing view.
- Direct visual inspection shows the complete head, torso, all four legs and feet, and the entire short black-tipped tail inside the frame with excellent isolation potential.
- Mandatory blocker: source metadata does **not explicitly establish adulthood or sex**. At 1543 px longest side it is also just below the preferred 1600 px target, though resolution alone would not disqualify a better fully verified adult.

A separate Flickr source explicitly labels an **adult male Eurasian lynx**, but it is All Rights Reserved and is a portrait rather than a strict full-body cutout source, so it cannot be redistributed or promoted.

**Result:** `SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED`. The new standing candidate substantially improves framing, but mandatory adulthood remains unverified and sex is unknown. No PNG created. **Macaw is next practical unresolved legacy audit.**
