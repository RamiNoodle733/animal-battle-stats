# Cross-roster recalibration checkpoint: 100 researched animals

**Scope:** staging research only through Hummingbird. This checkpoint reviews score inflation, inverted comparisons, compression, proportional-strength leakage, speed/agility confusion, double-counting, weak sourcing and duplicated ability concepts. It does not edit production data.

## Findings

1. **Absolute physical scaling remains intact.** Recent tiny animals show appropriately low Attack/Raw Power despite extraordinary proportional feats: Hercules Beetle 7.0 Attack, Hornet 10.0, Hummingbird 5.0. Medium mammals remain materially above them, while Hippopotamus at 90.0 Attack preserves the megafauna gap.
2. **Agility is not being treated as speed.** Howler Monkey receives high canopy maneuverability despite no certified maximum speed; Hummingbird's 98.0 Agility is grounded in hovering, reverse/lateral flight, abrupt braking, turning and aperture negotiation while `speed_mps` remains 0.0.
3. **Proportional-strength leakage is controlled.** Hercules Beetle's famous strength does not elevate it into vertebrate Raw Power bands. Hummingbird flight-muscle performance likewise remains 1.5 Raw Power.
4. **Special does not automatically track overall power.** Hippopotamus is physically overwhelming but only 69.0 Special; tiny Hornet and Hummingbird can exceed it in Special because venom or unusual flight/torpor are genuinely rare mechanisms.
5. **Unsupported PSI remains suppressed.** Recent reports explicitly retain 0.0 rather than converting force to pressure or copying web placeholders. This should continue roster-wide.
6. **No obvious inverted comparison requires a correction in the 75-100 tranche.** Harpy Eagle remains vastly above Hummingbird in absolute offense; Honey Badger remains above Howler Monkey in practical physical combat; Hippopotamus remains far above both; Hummingbird can nevertheless exceed them in Agility without contradiction.
7. **Stamina remains context-sensitive.** Low-metabolism survival is not automatically elite active endurance. Hummingbird migration earns high Stamina because sustained flight is directly documented, while Howler's rest-heavy folivorous budget does not.
8. **Duplicate ability concepts need naming discipline, not score correction.** Many flying animals will have flight-derived abilities. Future reports should name the specific mechanism or tactical function rather than reuse generic `Flight`, `Quick Reflexes`, `Tough Hide`, or `Survival Instinct` labels.
9. **Weak-source control remains important for lifespan and maximum speed.** Hummingbird's representative 5-year lifespan is explicitly moderate-low confidence and separated from its 9 y 2 mo record. Similar fields should remain flagged rather than given false precision.
10. **Image completion remains fully separate.** A source URL or verified adult photograph is not a transparent PNG. The verified-PNG count remains zero until actual binary RGBA files can be written and visually inspected.

## Corrections
No prior staging research file is changed by this checkpoint. The review found no comparison strong enough to justify a score correction without reopening the underlying species evidence. This is preferable to cosmetic normalization.

## Controls for animals 101-125
- Preserve large absolute gaps in Size, Raw Power, Weaponry and Attack.
- Keep `speed_mps=0.0` when a species-specific maximum is not defensible.
- Never convert Newtons to PSI without contact area.
- Keep Speed and Agility independent.
- Treat migration, low metabolism, torpor and environmental tolerance as distinct stamina/recovery phenomena.
- Do not score the same venom, armor, flight system or cognition advantage across three categories without distinct justification.
- Compare each new animal against at least one smaller, one similar and one larger researched peer where practical.
- Continue using exactly two named abilities and two unique traits, with mechanism-specific names.
- Keep image research and transparent-PNG completion as separate counts.
