## 2026-09-24 Peregrine Falcon legacy audit
Roster Peregrine Falcon is exact species ***Falco peregrinus***. Females are the larger fighting sex, but sex verification was not available for the strongest reusable flight candidate.

Broad discovery covered Google-style image search, Wikimedia Commons flight categories, USFWS, university/agency pages and Flickr-linked sources.

Strongest reusable candidate inspected:
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peregrine_Falcon_in_flight.jpg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/1/1e/Peregrine_Falcon_in_flight.jpg
- **3888×2592** real photograph of exact *Falco peregrinus* in flight at Morro Rock, California.
- Kevin Cole, originally Flickr; Commons FlickreviewR confirmed **CC BY 2.0**.
- Direct visual inspection of the original shows the full wing-to-wing and head-to-tail silhouette inside frame, with yellow talons visible.
- The page does **not** explicitly establish adulthood or sex. The source is therefore not promoted to the source-verified cutout backlog.

A separate University of Alaska/Geophysical Institute image is explicitly captioned **adult peregrine falcon in flight over Alaska** and visually shows a complete spread-wing silhouette and talons, but the page credits Ted Swem without a redistribution license. It is not committed or treated as reusable.

**Result:** SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED. No PNG created or claimed. **Piranha is next practical unresolved legacy audit.**
