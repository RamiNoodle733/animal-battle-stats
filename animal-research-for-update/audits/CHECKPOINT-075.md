# Cross-roster recalibration checkpoint: 75 research files

Scope: concise audit at 75/225 staging reports. This checkpoint does not alter production data.

## Findings

1. **Stat inflation:** recent small animals generally preserve absolute scaling. Fennec Fox/Ferret remain low Attack/Defense despite high maneuverability; Flying Squirrel and Gecko receive elite locomotor ratings without receiving mammal-scale Raw Power.
2. **Inverted comparisons:** no clear recent inversion requiring immediate correction. Coyote > Gazelle > tiny vertebrates in direct damaging power remains coherent, while Gaboon Viper legitimately breaks the mass hierarchy through venom.
3. **Compression:** physical scores retain large gaps from sub-kilogram specialists to 15–60 kg animals and then to 100+ kg animals. Continue guarding against mid-roster clustering around 40–60.
4. **Proportional-strength leakage:** Gecko is the main checkpoint test. Adhesive performance is confined primarily to Maneuverability/Abilities/Special, with Raw Power 7.0, Attack 12.0 and Defense 11.0.
5. **Speed/agility confusion:** recent profiles leave `speed_mps` at 0.0 when a defensible species maximum is absent, while Agility can still be high from measured/grounded control, adhesion, turning or evasiveness. This is the intended distinction.
6. **Double-counting:** Emperor Penguin cold/diving physiology, Gazelle desert adaptation and Gecko adhesion were checked for repeated scoring. Special is reserved for distinct mechanisms rather than simply echoing Stamina or Agility.
7. **Weak sources:** unsupported internet bite PSI continues to be rejected. Gecko's legacy 100 PSI and 36 km/h placeholders are not retained. Institutional or peer-reviewed context is preferred and uncertainty remains explicit.
8. **Duplicate ability concepts:** generic names such as “Survival Instinct” and “Quick Reflexes” should not propagate. Recent reports use species-grounded named mechanisms. Future audits should flag repeated generic “camouflage,” “speed burst,” or “power bite” concepts when a more biological name is possible.
9. **Image/research separation:** 75 research files do not imply 75 image assets. Verified committed transparent PNG count remains 0 because current connected GitHub writes are text-only.

## Corrections

No prior staging score was changed at this checkpoint. No sufficiently strong contradiction was found to justify opportunistic edits. The next checkpoint is due at **100 completed research files**.
