# ABS Research Calibration Checkpoint: 25 / 225

Date: 2026-09-17
Scope: first 25 completed staging research files, African Elephant through Black Bear. No live data changed.

## Checks performed

- Absolute scaling and pound-for-pound leakage
- Score inflation/compression and obvious inverted comparisons
- Speed being substituted for Agility
- Double-counted adaptations
- Unsupported bite-force PSI
- Duplicate ability concepts
- Weak-source extreme values
- Image-source status versus actual PNG completion

## Findings

1. **Physical-scale ordering is broadly coherent.** African Elephant remains the terrestrial high anchor among this tranche. Bison is clearly below it; Bighorn Sheep, Black Bear, Bactrian Camel and medium predators occupy lower absolute bands; tiny taxa such as Army Ant remain intentionally far below large vertebrates. No obvious pound-for-pound leakage requiring immediate score correction was identified.
2. **Agility is being separated from speed.** Bighorn Sheep's terrain control, Black Bear's climbing/forest maneuverability, and aerial taxa are justified independently from maximum speed. Bison's strong speed does not push it into small-animal agility territory.
3. **Bite-force discipline is good.** Reports commonly retain `bite_force_psi: 0.0` when only force in newtons, weak tertiary PSI claims, or no contact-area measurement exists. Continue this policy.
4. **Potential upper-band inflation remains a watch item.** African Elephant Attack 92.0 / Defense 94.0 and Albatross Stamina 96.0 are defensible provisional anchors but require comparison against later rhinoceros, hippopotamus, whales, extreme migrants and extinct giants. No immediate correction is warranted before those comparators exist.
5. **Stamina needs later normalization.** Arctic Wolf 86.0, Bactrian Camel 90.0, Beluga 84.0 and Albatross 96.0 reflect different endurance modes. A later audit should ensure migration, load carriage, diving and pursuit are not treated as interchangeable evidence.
6. **Special remains the category most vulnerable to double-counting.** Regeneration, echolocation, cold physiology and sensory specializations should affect Special only to the extent they create a distinct matchup mechanism rather than rewarding an adaptation already fully scored in Defense/Stamina/Senses.
7. **Ability names are distinct enough in this tranche.** Several abilities use environmental endurance or sensory themes, but no pair is a clear duplicate requiring renaming now.
8. **Image accounting was previously misleading.** All 25 text reports are research-complete, but verified committed full-body transparent PNG count is still **0**. `IMAGE_REQUIREMENTS.md` and `IMAGE_BACKFILL.md` now separate these counts. African Elephant has received the first retroactive full-body source audit.

## Recalibrations made at this checkpoint

- No prior numerical research file was changed solely to force normalization before adequate later comparators exist.
- African Elephant's report was rewritten/condensed during its mandatory image audit while retaining its established factual recommendations and headline calibration. Its image candidate was upgraded to a verified 6000×4000 adult-male *Loxodonta africana* source with reusable licensing.
