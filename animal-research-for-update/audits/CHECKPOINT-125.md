# Cross-roster recalibration checkpoint: 125 researched animals

**Scope:** staging research only through Manatee. This checkpoint reviews the required failure modes at the 125-report boundary: stat inflation, inverted comparisons, compression, proportional-strength leakage, speed/agility confusion, double-counting, weak sources and duplicate ability concepts. Production data is untouched.

## Findings
1. **Absolute physical scaling remains intact.** Tiny recent animals such as Magpie (15.0 Attack) and Macaw (24.0) remain far below Leopard (60.0), Jaguar (68.0) and megafauna controls. Lobster's strong proportional claw mechanics do not leak into mammal-scale Raw Power.
2. **Large mass is not automatically Attack.** The new 500 kg Manatee is only 36.0 Attack because it lacks dedicated killing weapons, while much smaller armed predators remain above it. This is a useful anti-size-double-counting control.
3. **Speed and Agility remain separate.** Manatee has an institutional 6.7 m/s short-burst value yet only 48.0 Agility. Magpie retains 84.0 Agility despite unresolved maximum speed because maneuverability has separate experimental support.
4. **Stamina distinctions are holding.** Leatherback's 95.0 reflects exceptional migration/diving physiology; Manatee 78.0 reflects telemetry-backed migration but acknowledges low metabolic output; Llama 77.0 reflects terrestrial pack/high-altitude endurance. Similar numbers are not being justified by the same mechanism.
5. **Unsupported bite PSI remains suppressed.** Recent Lobster, Lynx, Llama, Magpie and Manatee reports retain 0.0 where pressure is not defensible. Newtons are not converted to PSI without contact area.
6. **Special remains mechanism-specific.** Lionfish 87.0 venom, Leatherback 89.0 thermophysiology/diving, and Manatee 77.0 body-wide hydrodynamic touch represent distinct rare systems rather than general impressiveness.
7. **Cognition does not inflate physical stats.** Magpie 91.0 Intelligence remains paired with 15.0 Attack and 12.0 Defense. Macaw similarly retains low absolute physical ratings despite advanced cognition.
8. **No obvious inverted comparison in the 101-125 tranche warrants a score correction.** Leopard remains below Jaguar in Attack/Defense; Lynx remains below Leopard in absolute offense; Kudu exceeds smaller hoofed mammals in offense without reaching megafaunal bands; Manatee remains below armored Leatherback in Defense.
9. **Source-quality controls are working but representative lifespan and maximum-speed fields remain recurring weak points.** Reports correctly label uncertainty or use 0.0 rather than laundering tertiary values into measured facts. Continue preferring agency/peer-reviewed measurements and explicit context.
10. **Ability naming is mostly specific, but duplicate concepts remain a future editorial risk.** Flight, camouflage, migration, venom and sensory systems recur naturally across taxa. Names should continue describing species-specific mechanisms instead of generic `Flight`, `Strong Bite`, `Tough Hide` or `Enhanced Senses`.
11. **Image completion is still a separate blocked pipeline.** Multiple reusable adult full-body sources now exist, but 0 transparent PNG binaries are committed because the connected GitHub contents writer is UTF-8 text-only. No source URL is counted as a completed image.

## Corrections
No prior staging research file is changed by this checkpoint. The review found no comparison sufficiently inconsistent to justify reopening and changing a prior score without a species-specific evidence review. This avoids cosmetic normalization.

## Controls for animals 126-150
- Preserve large absolute gaps in Size, Raw Power, Weaponry, Attack and trauma resistance.
- Keep `speed_mps=0.0` where no defensible species-specific maximum exists; explicitly label burst, cruise, dive or migration speeds when available.
- Never convert force to PSI without defensible contact area.
- Keep Agility about acceleration/turning/control, not top speed.
- Separate long-distance migration, sustained high-output exercise, fasting tolerance, torpor and low metabolism when scoring Stamina.
- Prevent one adaptation from inflating Attack, Defense and Special simultaneously without distinct mechanisms.
- Keep cognition and sociality from multiplying individual physical force.
- Continue exactly two abilities and two traits with mechanism-specific names.
- Continue mandatory full-body image verification and keep source-verified candidates separate from actual transparent-PNG completion.
