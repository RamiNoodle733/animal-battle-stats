## 2026-09-22 Gray Wolf legacy audit
The existing Gray Wolf report's strongest reusable candidate remains Carlos Delgado's **5090x3054 CC BY-SA 4.0** exact Iberian-wolf photograph. It was previously visually verified as a complete single-wolf source with head, torso, all four legs/paws and tail visible, and the Commons categorization supports male sex. The unresolved requirement is explicit adulthood. This run searched broadly for a candidate that simultaneously resolves adult life stage, full-body framing, exact *Canis lupus* and reuse permission.

A **3000x2004 public-domain USFWS** image titled `Canis lupus standing in snow.jpg` was opened at original resolution. Although exact species and reuse are excellent, direct inspection shows the wolf emerging through deep snow/vegetation with the lower body, feet and tail not visibly complete. It therefore fails the strict anatomy rule and does not replace the existing candidate.

Source page: https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/File:Canis_lupus_standing_in_snow.jpg
Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c0/Canis_lupus_standing_in_snow.jpg
License: public domain, U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service
Visual result: exact gray wolf, but lower body/feet/tail are obscured

A separate **5365x3552** Photo12/imageBROKER source explicitly labels a European *Canis lupus* as an **adult**, standing, full body. Its license is rights-managed, so it is evidence that a suitable pose/life-stage photograph exists but it cannot be redistributed or committed without licensing.

Commercial source page: https://www.photo12.com/en/image/brk25d47_356
Result: **SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED** remains for the reusable candidate. Do not substitute the rights-managed adult source. Next practical audit: **Great Horned Owl**.

## 2026-09-22 Gorilla legacy audit
Broad search covered Google-style discovery, Wikimedia Commons, USFWS/public-domain material and photographer pages, while preserving the existing report's exact canonical taxon: mountain gorilla *Gorilla beringei beringei*. Western-lowland silverback images were rejected as wrong taxon even when high resolution.

The strongest exact-taxon life-stage candidate found is Dave Proffer's **1552x1632 CC BY 2.0** photograph. Commons explicitly identifies it as an **adult male Mountain gorilla** and places it in the mountain-gorilla category.
