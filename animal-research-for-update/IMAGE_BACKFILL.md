## 2026-09-20 Hercules Beetle legacy audit
Broad search covered Google-style image discovery, Wikimedia Commons live-animal and specimen categories, Smithsonian material, photographer/stock discovery and exact-species sources. Museum specimens were not accepted as substitutes for a healthy living adult.

**Materially stronger living candidate:**
- Source page: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hercules_beetle_(Dynastes_hercules).jpg
- Original image URL: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/51/Hercules_beetle_%28Dynastes_hercules%29.jpg
- Original resolution: **4000x2248**
- Taxon: exact Hercules beetle, *Dynastes hercules*
- Life stage: **adult/imago verified by source narrative**, which says that after two years of waiting the animal had just emerged/turned into a beetle. In a holometabolous beetle, the imago is the adult stage.
- Sex: the source narrative uses male pronouns; horn morphology is consistent with a male, but this audit does not infer sex solely from appearance.
- Provenance/license: Novita Estiti Flickr photograph, **CC BY 2.0**, independently confirmed by Commons FlickreviewR on 2 April 2013.
- Resolution/provenance result: this is substantially better than the previously queued 5861x3853 museum specimen because it is a real living adult and has clear reusable provenance.
- Strict anatomy result: the source is retained as the new primary candidate, but the accessible inspection in this run did not allow confident certification that every one of the six feet and all appendage tips are cleanly visible and unobscured. The image therefore remains below the mandatory whole-animal verification threshold.
- Status: **SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED**. No PNG is claimed.

**Other candidates checked:**
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dynastes_hercules_m.jpg - 3388x1365, CC BY-SA 3.0, explicit male *D. h. lichyi*, but a stacked collection specimen rather than the preferred healthy living adult.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dynastes_hercules.lichyi_(male).JPG - 1740x3690, CC BY-SA 3.0, explicit 144 mm male from Peru, but a specimen image.
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dynastes_hercules_(Cahuita).jpg - 2191x2921, CC BY-SA 4.0, exact species photographed in Cahuita National Park; life-stage/strict complete anatomy metadata remains weaker than the new candidate.
- Smithsonian exact-*D. hercules* male collection record is CC0/public domain but is collection material, not a living healthy-adult photograph.
- Commercial/stock living photographs were found, but unknown or commercial redistribution rights make them unsuitable for committing as staging assets.

**Outcome:** Hercules Beetle advances from a museum-specimen-centered candidate to a high-resolution, reusable, exact-species living adult source. It is not promoted to `FULL-BODY SOURCE FOUND` until every foot and appendage tip can be visually certified. **Hippopotamus** is the next unresolved legacy source-quality audit.
