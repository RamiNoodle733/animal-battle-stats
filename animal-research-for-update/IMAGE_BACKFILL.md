## Latest findings

### 2026-09-19 Flamingo legacy audit

Broad re-search covered general web image discovery, Wikimedia Commons and adult-labelled sources for the report's resolved exact taxon, Greater Flamingo (*Phoenicopterus roseus*).

A seemingly excellent new Commons result, `Greater Flamingo in SA.jpg`, was described as **Adult in flight**, **4148x2765**, own work by Jakdewet, and **CC BY-SA 4.0**. It would have been attractive because an adult flight photograph can expose the complete wings, legs and feet. Direct page inspection, however, revealed a decisive provenance/taxonomy failure: the title currently redirects to **`Lesser Flamingo in South Africa.jpg`**, and Commons categorizes the image under *Phoeniconaias minor*. It is therefore the wrong species for this report and was rejected rather than trusted from its misleading search title/snippet.

Other explicitly adult sources were found, including an ADW adult/sexually mature *P. roseus* photograph and an `Adult Flamingo at Ras Al Khor Wildlife Sanctuary` Commons file, but they either do not provide a clean complete-body terrestrial silhouette or fall below the preferred 1600-pixel quality target. Group images were also rejected under the single-animal standard.

The existing report candidate remains the stronger exact-species option:
- **Source page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:A_flamingo_standing_in_Lake_Poyrazlar.jpg
- **Original image:** https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/a4/A_flamingo_standing_in_Lake_Poyrazlar.jpg
- **Resolution:** 6000x4000
- **Taxon:** *Phoenicopterus roseus*
- **License:** CC BY-SA 4.0
- **Status:** `SOURCE FOUND - FULL-BODY NOT VERIFIED`

The prior report already records this source page and original image URL. It is retained rather than replaced by a lower-quality or wrong-species candidate. Adulthood is not explicitly established and strict every-foot/toe completeness remains unverified, so no PNG was produced and no completion was claimed.
